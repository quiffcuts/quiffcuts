Quiff Cuts, 61–62 Makkah Commercial Market, 208 Chak Road, Eden Valley, Model City 1, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan, is responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us on +92 308 2270007 or at info@quiffcuts.com.

2. What we collect

Information Why we need it
Your name To know whose appointment it is.
Mobile number To confirm the booking and tell you if anything changes.
Date, time and service To keep the chair and the barber ready.
Your note (optional) Only what you choose to write.

We do not ask for your address, ID number, date of birth or any card details.

3. Where it is kept

Bookings are stored in Google Firebase. Access is password-protected.

A WhatsApp message is opened on your own phone; you send it from your own account.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking records for up to 24 months, then delete them.

5. Who else can see it

Only the owner and the salon manager. We never sell or share your information for marketing.

6. Cookies and tracking

We use no advertising cookies. Your browser only stores your language choice.

Fonts, icons and the map are loaded from Google and Cloudflare.

7. Your rights

  • Ask us what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your booking record.
  • Ask us not to contact you again.

Message us or email info@quiffcuts.com and we will act within 7 days, free of charge.

This website is not intended for children under 13.
