Payment & pricing

Everything you pay for is listed before you sit down.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Currency

All prices on this website are shown in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

2. When you pay

Payment is made at the salon, after your service is finished.

3. Accepted payment methods

4. Receipts

A printed receipt is issued for every payment.

5. Prices and changes

Where a price is shown as a range, your barber confirms the exact figure before starting.

We may revise our prices from time to time; this website always shows the current ones.

6. Packages

Package prices are fixed and paid at the salon.

7. Online card payment

At present this website does not take payment online.

8. Questions about a charge

If you think you were charged incorrectly, contact us within 7 days.
